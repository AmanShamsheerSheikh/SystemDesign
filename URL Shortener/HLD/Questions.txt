## URL Shortener — Self-Check Questions for Revision

Use these cold, without looking back at the thread. If you can answer all of these without hesitating, you've actually retained the reasoning, not just the diagram.

**Traffic estimation**
1. Given monthly write/read counts, walk through the full chain to peak QPS — name every step, don't skip to the answer.
2. Why do you apply a peak multiplier at all? Why might 2x be wrong for this specific system?
3. What's the actual error if you size a component off average QPS instead of peak? Why does it matter *which direction* the error goes?

**Component sizing — the general pattern**
4. State the three-step process that applies to sizing *any* component, before naming a single number.
5. For each of these, what's the "load that actually reaches it," and why is it filtered differently: App Server, Redis, DB Replica, DB Master?
6. Is cache hit rate calculated or assumed? Why does that distinction matter to how you present it out loud?

**Redis**
7. Why does Redis's per-instance ceiling differ so drastically from Postgres's? Name the actual mechanism, not just "it's faster."
8. At very-high volume, is one Redis node "enough"? Under what framing does that answer flip?
9. If someone challenges "you're under capacity, how could it still go down" — what's your answer, precisely?
10. What's the tradeoff of write-through caching on link creation? What's the middle-ground fix, and why does it work?

**Database**
11. Why do reads never go to Master, even though Master technically *could* serve them?
12. What's the one real exception where reading from Master (or an equivalent workaround) is justified?
13. Why does Master stay at 1 far longer than any other component? What's structurally different about scaling it?
14. What's the escalation order when Master write volume actually becomes a problem, and why that order?
15. If sharding, what would you shard by, and why does that choice matter for the read path specifically?
16. What actually happens to existing data when you naively reshard (`% num_shards`) — and what technique avoids it?

**Load Balancer**
17. Why doesn't the standard `load/capacity` formula meaningfully apply to sizing load balancers?
18. What are the two real reasons to run more than one LB, and are either of them about throughput?

**Write path & consistency**
19. In the write flow, does order matter between writing to Master and writing to Redis? What breaks if you get it backwards?
20. What does the arrow from Master to Read Replica represent, and why does it need to be visually distinct from other arrows in the diagram?
21. Describe the exact failure scenario that read-after-write inconsistency causes for a real user.

**Design process**
22. Should Redis appear in your diagram from the first box, or should you earn it? What's the actual reasoning move that justifies adding it?
23. At medium volume, most components round to 1-2. What's the correct thing to say out loud when that happens, instead of just presenting the numbers?

If any of these make you pause and reach for the thread instead of reasoning it out live, that's your revision list — not the diagram itself.
